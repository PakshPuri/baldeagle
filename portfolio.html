Welcome to Team Kinetix

About Team Kinetix

Team Kinetix is a collective of passionate student engineers, makers, and innovators driven by a relentless curiosity for robotics, embedded systems, and advanced hardware prototyping. Based out of Delhi NCR, we don't just study technology—we build it from the ground up.

From competitive combat robots and precision line-followers to autonomous navigation systems and custom circuit board designs, our work spans mechanical fabrication, custom electronics, and robust algorithmic control. Every project we undertake is a test of engineering discipline, rigorous real-world testing, and iterative design.

We believe that the future of engineering belongs to those who build with intent. Team Kinetix is our shared platform to push boundaries, tackle complex technical challenges, and engineer solutions that perform on competitive stages.

Projects

Ares

A 5kg-class RoboSumo combat robot built within 25×25×25cm guidelines, featuring a 6-wheeled Johnson motor drivetrain and a front wedge that slides under opponents and drives them out. Placed 3rd at PRC Jasola.

Velocity V3

A solo-built line follower that placed 3rd at TechRadiance, IIT Delhi — on a first LFR attempt. Built across three versions under strict constraints.

Cosmo

A cheerful purple desk robot hand-crafted with a 3D pen. Uses OpenCV face recognition to identify people and waves hello through a hinged arm mechanism.

Hermes

A 5kg-class RoboRace bot with an AWD 4-Johnson-motor drivetrain, tuned to 600RPM. Custom-cut motor shafts reduce weight and improve handling. Top 5 at PRC Jasola.

Epimetheus V2

Epimetheus V2 features 2000RPM N20 motors, an Arduino Nano, TB6612 motor driver, 8-channel IR sensor array, active downforce impeller, and a full PCB chassis design.

DodgeX

A retro pixel-art dodge game controlled entirely by hand gestures using Python, OpenCV, and MediaPipe for real-time lane switching.

Arduino Radar

A real-time proximity radar using an ultrasonic sensor, visualised through Python. Built without a servo, the sweep was painstakingly tuned by hand.

PocketRC

A Bluetooth-controlled RC car with variable speed control, operated through a custom Android app built with an Arduino Nano.

Unity

A static website for a competition built in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript around the theme of unity and diversity. Placed Top 5.

Theseus

An autonomous maze-solving robot using flood fill and shortest path algorithms on an Arduino Nano, with a fully 3D printed chassis.

Icarus

A specialized robosoccer bot engineered from the chassis architecture of Ares, featuring a redesigned angled wedge optimized for precise ball control, striking, and maneuverability.
